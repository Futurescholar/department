Academic calendar and rules

These settings control the whole portal: which session and semester courses are registered for, whether students can register right now, unit limits, and the GPA levels that trigger a warning on a student's page.

  • Warning level: a student whose semester GPA or CGPA is at or below this sees a red warning.
  • Watch level: at or below this (but above the warning level) shows an amber notice.
